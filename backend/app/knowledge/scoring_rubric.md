# Default Essay Scoring Rubric

Every essay is scored on four dimensions. Each dimension receives an integer score from 1 to 10.
The overall score is the weighted combination: Grammar & Mechanics 20%, Coherence & Structure 25%,
Content & Relevance 35%, Vocabulary & Style 20%.

## Grammar & Mechanics (weight 20%)

Grammar & Mechanics measures grammar, spelling, punctuation and sentence correctness.

- Grammar & Mechanics score 9-10 (Excellent): Virtually error-free. Sentences are grammatically correct and varied. Spelling and punctuation are accurate throughout. Any slip is minor and does not distract the reader.
- Grammar & Mechanics score 7-8 (Good): A few minor errors in grammar, spelling or punctuation. Errors do not interfere with meaning. Sentence construction is generally sound.
- Grammar & Mechanics score 5-6 (Adequate): Noticeable errors, such as subject-verb agreement, tense shifts, comma splices or misspellings, appear regularly. Meaning is still clear but the errors distract.
- Grammar & Mechanics score 3-4 (Weak): Frequent errors in most paragraphs. Run-on sentences or fragments are common. Errors sometimes obscure meaning.
- Grammar & Mechanics score 1-2 (Very weak): Errors in almost every sentence. Meaning is often unclear because of grammar, spelling or punctuation problems.

## Coherence & Structure (weight 25%)

Coherence & Structure measures logical organization, paragraph structure and transitions.

- Coherence & Structure score 9-10 (Excellent): Clear introduction with a thesis, well-developed body paragraphs each built around one main idea, and a conclusion that follows from the argument. Ideas progress logically and transitions are smooth and varied.
- Coherence & Structure score 7-8 (Good): Clear overall structure with introduction, body and conclusion. Most paragraphs have a clear focus. Transitions are present but sometimes mechanical or missing.
- Coherence & Structure score 5-6 (Adequate): A basic structure is recognisable but uneven. Some paragraphs mix several ideas or lack topic sentences. Transitions are limited and the order of ideas is sometimes hard to follow.
- Coherence & Structure score 3-4 (Weak): Weak organization. Introduction or conclusion is missing or ineffective. Ideas jump without connection. Paragraphing is poor or absent.
- Coherence & Structure score 1-2 (Very weak): No discernible structure. The essay reads as a list of unrelated statements or a single undivided block with no logical order.

## Content & Relevance (weight 35%)

Content & Relevance measures relevance to the prompt, argument quality and supporting evidence.

- Content & Relevance score 9-10 (Excellent): Fully addresses every part of the prompt with a clear, insightful position. Arguments are well reasoned and supported by specific, relevant examples or evidence. Shows depth and may address counter-arguments.
- Content & Relevance score 7-8 (Good): Addresses the prompt with a clear position. Arguments are relevant and mostly supported by examples, though some support is general or underdeveloped.
- Content & Relevance score 5-6 (Adequate): Addresses the prompt but in a general or partial way. The position may be unclear at times. Supporting points are thin, repetitive or asserted without evidence.
- Content & Relevance score 3-4 (Weak): Only loosely related to the prompt. Few relevant ideas, little or no supporting evidence, or significant factual or logical problems.
- Content & Relevance score 1-2 (Very weak): Off-topic, does not answer the prompt, or contains almost no relevant content.

## Vocabulary & Style (weight 20%)

Vocabulary & Style measures word choice, vocabulary richness, tone and writing style.

- Vocabulary & Style score 9-10 (Excellent): Precise, varied and sophisticated vocabulary used accurately. Tone is consistent and appropriate for an academic essay. Sentence style is varied and engaging.
- Vocabulary & Style score 7-8 (Good): Good range of vocabulary with mostly accurate word choice. Tone is appropriate. Some variety in sentence style, with occasional repetition.
- Vocabulary & Style score 5-6 (Adequate): Adequate but limited vocabulary. Common words are repeated and some word choices are imprecise. Tone is mostly appropriate but may slip into informal language.
- Vocabulary & Style score 3-4 (Weak): Basic, repetitive vocabulary. Frequent wrong or vague word choices. Tone is often inappropriate or inconsistent.
- Vocabulary & Style score 1-2 (Very weak): Very limited vocabulary that prevents ideas from being expressed. Word choice errors are pervasive.
